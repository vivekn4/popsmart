# POPSMART — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 18, 2026

POPSMART is a micro-learning app by VN Thoughts. This policy explains what the app does with your information — in short: **nothing leaves your device.**

## Data we collect

**None.** POPSMART does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no network requests of any kind.

## Data stored on your device

Everything the app remembers — your lesson progress, XP, badges, stickers, streaks, and settings — is stored locally on your device using the app's private storage. It never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it permanently.

## Permissions

POPSMART requests no sensitive Android permissions. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files.

## Children

The app contains only educational content and is safe for all ages. Since no data is collected from anyone, there is nothing further to disclose under child-privacy regulations.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.
